Section 01

Brand Foundation

Lutecia Care is a leading independent nuclear-medicine platform in France — founded in 2024, majority-owned by its founders and physicians, dedicated to excellence in PET-CT and scintigraphy. The brand holds two ideas in tension and balance: clinical authority and human care.

Mission

A model built around physicians and patients

A specialised platform that structures, coordinates and develops a network of nuclear-medicine centres — on strong medical expertise and a strategic partnership with independent physicians.

Positioning

The national leader, dedicated to one thing

A pure-play in nuclear medicine — not a generalist imaging group. Depth over breadth: best-in-class infrastructure, rapid adoption of innovation including theranostics, a national footprint.

Section 02

Personality & Values

Three words anchor every decision — Excellence, precision, access. They appear verbatim in the hero and should guide tone, layout, imagery and product.

01

Authoritative, not cold

Serif headlines and deep navy convey institutional gravity; generous whitespace and warm off-white keep it humane.

02

Precise, never busy

Thin 1.4–1.5px linework, restrained accents, one idea per block — design that mirrors diagnostic precision.

03

Premium, but accessible

Editorial typography signals quality; plain bilingual language and clear journeys keep it open to all.

✓ We are

Independent & physician-led

Majority-owned by founders and physicians; a partner, not an employer.

Specialist — pure-play nuclear medicine

Scientific & innovation-forward

Calm, considered, editorial

Quiet confidence over noise; whitespace over density.

Bilingual by default

✗ We are not

A generic hospital or radiology chain

We don't look or speak like a commodity imaging provider.

Loud, salesy or jargon-heavy to patients

Cluttered or trend-chasing

Section 03

Logo & Wordmark

The logo is an SVG wordmark — "Lutecia / Care" set in Cormorant Garamond on two lines, framed by an open, asymmetric bracket of 1.4px strokes. The bracket is unclosed by design: it reads as a frame, a viewport, an aperture — never a box. It scales cleanly and inherits its colour from currentColor.

Section 11

Animation & Motion

Restrained and consistent. One easing curve governs nearly everything; entrances are a gentle fade-and-rise. Movement should feel like a held breath, never a bounce.

Timing tokens
Standard transition0.35s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)
Entrance (fade-rise)0.6s ease · Y 24→0
Stagger0.1s steps · delay-1…5
Hover lifttranslateY(-1 to -2px)
Ambient drift12–20s linear/alt
Principles
1

One curve

Use the standard ease for colour, transform and shadow. Consistency reads as quality.

2

Reveal once

Elements fade-and-rise as they enter, then stay. No looping, no scroll-jacking.

3

Ambient, not loud

The hero gradient drifts and particles float slowly. Background life — never a distraction.

Accessibility. Honour prefers-reduced-motion: disable gradient drift, particle float and entrance transforms, leaving instant opacity. Functional hovers can remain.
Section 12

Imagery & Backgrounds

In place of stock photography, the brand's hero uses a living navy gradient overlaid with a fine particle grid — evoking a scan field, a constellation, depth without literalism. This is the most distinctive visual asset; protect it.

Nuclear Medicine
Excellence, precision
and access to care

Gradient recipe

Two radial glows (navy-mid @ 70/40, navy-dark @ 20/70) over a 135° navy-dark→navy→navy-dark base. Subtle 12s hue drift.

Particle field

Two dotted radial patterns (steel @ 12%, mauve @ 8%) at 60px & 100px tiles, drifting 20s linear. Barely-there texture.

If using photography

Real clinical environments & equipment, cool-toned, clean and bright, with a navy overlay so it sits in the palette. Never warm or stocky.

Don't. No off-brand bright/warm gradients · no literal radioactive/hazard iconography · no dense text over the busiest part of the field.
Section 13

Brand Voice & Tone

Assured, warm, bilingual. Lutecia Care speaks with quiet confidence — precise enough for physicians, reassuring enough for patients. Every public string ships in English and French as equals (the site toggles via data-en / data-fr).

Confident, not boastful

  • · State facts; let numbers carry the claim
  • · No superlatives, no marketing buzzwords
DO: "7 centres · >33k PET-CT exams · €c.40m revenue."
NOT: "The most powerful nuclear-medicine platform ever built!"

Human, not clinical

  • · Patient copy is plain, calm, reassuring
  • · Explain; don't bury in jargon
DO: "As clear and comfortable as possible."
NOT: "Radiopharmaceutical administration per protocol."

Precise, not vague

  • · Specifics, credentials, dates
  • · Short sentences, active voice, the em dash for rhythm
DO: "Authorisation granted Sep. 2025; opening autumn 2027."
NOT: "Coming soon to a new region."

Bilingual, not bolt-on

  • · Write both languages from the start
  • · Adapt idiom; use « guillemets » & French spacing
EN: "A leading independent player in nuclear medicine."
FR: « Un acteur indépendant de référence en médecine nucléaire. »
Terminology map. PET-CT ⇄ TEP-TDM · Scintigraphy ⇄ Scintigraphie · γ-camera ⇄ γ-caméra · Physicians ⇄ Médecins · Theranostics ⇄ Théranostique. Keep consistent everywhere.
Section 14

Do's & Don'ts

The fast reference for applying the brand consistently across every surface.

✓ Do

Lead with navy & neutrals

The brand lives on deep navy and warm off-white. Let mauve appear only as emphasis.

Lutecia Care

Use the serif for headings, sans for everything else

Cormorant for display and figures; Inter for body, UI and labels. Never mix the roles.

Turn the key phrase italic-mauve

In a heading, set the one phrase that matters in italic serif — it renders mauve. One accent per heading.

Keep the bracket open

The logo frame is intentionally unclosed. Preserve the asymmetry and the two-line lockup.

Write EN & FR together

Both languages, equal weight, from the first draft.

✗ Don't

Don't flood with mauve

Mauve is a 10% seasoning. Large mauve fields cheapen the palette and kill the accent.

Too much accent

Don't set the wordmark in sans

In Inter or Arial it looks like a different company. The Cormorant serif treatment is non-negotiable.

Lutecia Care

Don't close or distort the bracket

No boxing the logo, no stretch/skew/rotate, no shadows or gradients on the mark.

Don't use bold serif headings

Light/regular is the voice. Bold Cormorant reads heavy and off-brand.

Don't bolt French on at the end

And never mix both languages inside one rendered string.
